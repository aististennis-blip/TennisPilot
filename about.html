THE IDEA

A bridge between coaching sessions.

TennisPilot is designed to help players organize their development and give coaches better information to work with.

⚠️

The problem

Players consume lots of tennis information, but often don't know what to practice this week, what matters most in matches, or how to turn a bad result into a useful training decision.

💡

The solution

TennisPilot creates a repeatable loop: assess → plan → train → play → review → discuss with coach → adjust.

The six-step loop

1

Assess

Create a simple snapshot of the player's game.

2

Plan

Choose one clear weekly priority with the coach.

3

Train

Track a few focused sessions instead of everything.

4

Play

Use matches as evidence, not just wins and losses.

5

Review

Record recurring problems and what worked.

6

Adjust

Bring the pattern to the coach and update the plan.

Why a coach could care

Better conversations

Players arrive with specific observations instead of vague complaints.

More continuity

The plan between lessons stays connected to the coach's priorities.

Clearer patterns

Repeated match problems can become easier to notice over time.
